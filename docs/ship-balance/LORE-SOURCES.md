# Lore and role review — 13 September 2026

This is a Broken Mirror gameplay roster, not a canon fleet database. Approved
factions, civilian conversions, artwork and distinct variants remain authoritative.
Canonical classes inform roles; the prices, health, cargo, speed, standing and
three-slot fits are authored game balance. The slots represent equipment groups,
not a literal count of a starship's gun mounts.

No authoritative class identification was found for many BM-specific names.
Their original pack descriptions and the user's review govern those records.
Names such as Concord, Delpin, Promelli, Thawn and the Project X Excalibur are
not presented as newly verified canonical classes. The Excalibur supercapital is
not the Ambassador- or Constitution-class ship that happens to share that name.
The mixed-era imports remain deliberate BM adaptations; they do not establish
that the canon timeline or BM2's Dominion-victory scenario happened in this game.

EAS is a secondary reference with its own interpretations; uncertain details
are not elevated to official canon. No artwork was downloaded from these sources.

## Mechanics deliberately not promised by a role label

- Science equipment here means available tachyon/utility equipment and travel
  capacity, not a new science mission or sensor simulation.
- An Olympic does not automatically heal ships. A tractor-equipped hull does
  have the existing tractor device. No carrier launch system was added.
- Crossfield has no spore drive and Prometheus has no multi-vector separation.
- Extended reserves on organic, quantum-drive and sailing ships do not mean
  free antimatter or passive refueling. Those abilities need a separate patch.
- The existing weapon catalog provides the three-slot abstractions. Its weapon
  names and damage figures were not rewritten to claim canon accuracy.
