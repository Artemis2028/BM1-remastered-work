# Ship power and crew management — implementation and review

Base: `302639ac62c615406d0222063685bf969ff38b88` (pushed main, reviewed ships and prices). This is a separate incremental patch. It does not reinstall the ship art pack or alter hull IDs, prices, standing, approved weapon fits, regions, ownership, or ROE.

## Implemented

- Every one of the 172 active hulls has an authored reactor output, energy capacity, cruise demand, shield-recovery demand, shield-recovery rate and cloak demand. `bm-ships/ships.json` is authoritative; the compatibility manifest is generated by `scripts/sync-ship-roster.mjs`. See `HULL-POWER-VALUES.md`.
- Player ships, escorts, garrisons and other NPC ships use `src/ship-power.mjs`. Generation and energy capacity are independent. Skill, faction and target do not multiply generation, reduce costs, or award damage bonuses.
- Movement, shield recovery, weapon activations and cloak upkeep use the same reserve. Full shields do not incur recovery demand; recent hits still delay recovery. Under supply shortage, recovery yields to propulsion, propulsion output is reduced if necessary, and an unaffordable cloak disengages.
- The existing 20-point OPS allocation remains. Five engine points provide normal impulse; ten provide 130%; zero disables powered motion. Accelerating into in-system warp increases drive demand. The flight tick now respects engine allocation, instead of overwriting it. NPC normal and ambient-warp movement also respect supplied engine power.
- Weapons allocation scales shot power and its energy cost together. NPC cost depends on its own hull and allocation, including when the player uses the same hull. Energy denial happens before cooldown, projectile, sound or attack evidence. Failed player engine-disruptor/Thaleron attempts and invalid tractor targets do not consume energy.
- Ship shields now recover the same percentage per second for the same hull and allocation. Player percentage pools and NPC absolute combat pools are converted at the adapter. This deliberately replaces the previous NPC flat 0.85-point recharge; large NPCs can recover more shields than before, but must pay for it. Stations retain their existing separate regeneration and turret rules.
- OPS shows reactor output, reserves, consumption and net recovery/drain. Weapon consumption uses exponential smoothing with a one-second time constant; actual energy debits are immediate and exact. Full reserves show surplus generation. Ship purchase details show reactor output and storage.
- The supplied Flash description's secondary Warp Core was not an available runtime item on this base. This patch adds **Secondary Warp Core, item 31**, at 12,000 latinum, stocked by Terran, Vulcan, Ferengi and neutral vendors. It is a passive installed Device in one of the existing three slots. One or more fitted cores doubles output once, never capacity or current charge; inventory ownership alone does nothing. It is not added to any starting fit.

## Four crew skills, separate temperament

Crew profiles are selected deterministically at creation, remain with the vessel, and can be authored with `crewSkill` and `crewTemperament` in `createNpcShip`. Government patrols lean regular/veteran; pirates lean inexperienced/regular. Both allow rare exceptions. Raising a flag does not reroll a crew.

| Skill | Decision interval | Base reserve floor | Recover to | Forecast of existing net drain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Inexperienced | 3.0 s | 1% | 10% | 0 s |
| Regular | 1.6 s | 12% | 30% | 0.5 s |
| Veteran | 0.8 s | 22% | 48% | 1.5 s |
| Elite | 0.35 s | 30% | 60% | 2.5 s |

These intervals use elapsed simulation time. Profiles decide how often to reconsider allocations and when to pace fire or enter recovery. Recovery has hysteresis: it lasts until the higher recovery threshold. A ship already below its floor does not shoot its way out of trouble.

Temperament offsets both reserve thresholds: reckless -10 percentage points, disciplined 0, cautious +10, desperate -18, bounded to valid values. Desperate is available for authored encounters; no new automatic desperation or morale system is implied. The default temperament selection is independent of skill; pirate crews more often lean reckless. Crew decisions use their own reserves, shield condition, known weapon cost and whether the existing targeting code found an engagement. They do not inspect enemy reserves, equipment or hidden contacts.

Default skill weights, inexperienced/regular/veteran/elite:

- Pirate vessels: 50% / 36% / 12% / 2%.
- Non-neutral, non-pirate military roles: 8% / 52% / 35% / 5%.
- Other ships: 30% / 55% / 14% / 1%.

These are initial tuning values, not lore assertions. Hull strength and pilot skill remain independent.

## Persistence boundaries

- Player charge and allocations persist through save/load.
- Fleet charge, allocations, recovery state, skill and temperament persist through travel, cache recreation and save/load. Saved fleet power is not overwritten by stale live NPCs when loading.
- Existing ordinary cached ships keep charge and crew across return and change of holder. Ambient replacement uses a new vessel seed and fresh charge/crew, never the previous slot occupant's state.
- Active checkpoint participant snapshots carry charge and crew through their existing sanitization/restoration path.
- The base game still regenerates ordinary ambient populations when loading a save or explicitly rebuilding a scene. This patch does not introduce full-world NPC persistence or offline simulation. Ordinary regenerated contacts are not represented as saved persistent ships. Off-screen fleets do not gain simulated recharge.

## Boundaries and next patch

The existing NPC first-compatible-combat-weapon selector and difficulty damage/cooldown scales remain. All current ship shots now pay energy, but this is not a new multi-slot NPC firing AI, NPC device-use AI, retreat planner or fleet formation controller. Skill affects allocation, pacing and recovery. Station reactors, additional antimatter burn, reactor damage, training purchases and engineering upgrades are not introduced here.

The next system is passive/active sensors, followed by upgradeable sensor suites and EW. Detection, identification and weapons lock must be separate. Cargo ships may be refitted for reconnaissance through installed equipment, power allocation and capacity tradeoffs. A hull's role does not prohibit refitting. Base sensors/reactors are integral; flags and passes remain separate from the three weapon/device slots. The capacity/cost details of future optional engineering modules are not implemented by this patch.

The new pure model suite checks accounting, brownouts, allocation, core behavior, crew distributions, reaction intervals, recovery hysteresis and serialization. The live suite boots the real module in Chromium and checks actual weapon debits, failed-shot evidence, player/NPC parity, cloak, flight tick, cached return, fleet save/load, checkpoint snapshot fields, all 172 profile adapters and OPS rendering.

Sustained-combat experiment: four otherwise identical Alien Bioships start with full energy, 15% shields and a combat engagement, with the same disciplined temperament. For 180 simulated seconds they use the real `advanceActorPower` and `fireNpcWeapon` paths against the same durable fixture. No weapon cooldown or reactor multiplier is changed for the test.

| Crew | Lowest reserve | Shots | Time below 10% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Inexperienced | 1.02% | 77 | 95.3 s |
| Regular | 12.03% | 78 | 0 s |
| Veteran | 22.02% | 77 | 0 s |
| Elite | 30.01% | 76 | 0 s |

This establishes different reserve behavior under a controlled load. It does not establish a universal win-rate ordering or final campaign balance. Combat pacing, stronger paid NPC shield recovery and the 12,000-latinum core deserve playtesting.

The existing S1d attribution fixture now holds its one-hit target stationary and begins the normal shield-regeneration delay before launch. A final regression run exposed an unrelated evasive-movement timing failure; the assertion still requires the real NPC-owned torpedo, playerEscort credit and actual kill rewards. No acceptance assertion was removed.
