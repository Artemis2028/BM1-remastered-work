Recommended interpretation: rate the ship's sustained strategic cruise—the pace it can actually maintain between systems without treating emergency maximum warp as normal travel. Canon is inconsistent, so these recommendations use on-screen/official speed statements where available, then role/era inference for BM/custom hulls. Exotic propulsion such as Borg transwarp or Crossfield spore drive should be a separate movement mechanic rather than a giant 1–10 score.
Research basis & how I scored recommendations

High-confidence anchors: Intrepid has a canon maximum cruising speed of warp 9.975; Prometheus is described around warp 9.9/9.99 and as Starfleet's fastest in its era; Galaxy can reach ~9.6 sustained for hours / 9.8 at extreme risk; Defiant reaches about 9.5 but its drive is explicitly overpowered for the hull; Nova tops out at warp 8; Danube runabouts top out at warp 5; D'deridex is observed around warp 9, can hit 9.6 at propulsion cost, and is limited to warp 6 if it wants to remain cloaked; Vor'cha is listed at warp 9.5+; Constitution III is observed at warp 9.99; Inquiry is explicitly called Starfleet's fastest ship in 2399.

Why this is not a warp-factor table: Star Trek's warp scale is nonlinear and dialogue is inconsistent. “Maximum,” “cruising,” and “sustainable” are also not interchangeable. For BM1 the useful gameplay quantity is sustained point-to-point strategic performance.

Community interpretation: fan/Daystrom discussions commonly distinguish sprint speed from sustained cruise. This is especially important for Intrepid/Prometheus/Sovereign comparisons, and for ships like Defiant whose engines are unusually powerful but stress the hull. Borg transwarp is likewise better represented as a special routing/travel capability than a simple faster conventional warp number.

Low-confidence BM/custom hulls: recommendations are deliberately provisional, based on stated role, era cues, class, and the roster's own description. Use your rating to override them.

Source set used for the recommendations: Memory Alpha class / maximum-warp pages for Sovereign, Intrepid, Prometheus, Galaxy, Defiant, Nova, Danube, D'deridex, Vor'cha, Negh'Var, Constitution III, Inquiry, Galor/Keldon, Jem'Hadar craft, D'Kora/Ferengi Marauder and related classes; plus r/startrek and r/DaystromInstitute discussions on sustained vs maximum warp, Sovereign/Intrepid/Prometheus roles, D'deridex limitations, and Borg transwarp.
