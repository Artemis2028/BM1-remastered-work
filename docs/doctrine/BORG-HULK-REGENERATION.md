# BORG HULK REGENERATION / RECOVERY

Stacked on the accepted, frozen captain-Borg tip `e949936997a9d6b730ad70b1044b12b208def8a0`. Everything accepted through it is preserved. Nothing has been pushed, merged or deployed.

Until now, a hull the Collective assimilated while it was disabled lay where it was taken for ever. No Borg yard exists, so the ordinary repair rules never reached it.

It is now brought in and regenerated by a loop of the Collective's own. That loop obeys the systems already built:
- **Movement:** the shared journey system — real legs, ship speed, crossings, blockades, save/load.
- **Towing:** the shared tow mission — the 0.70 penalty, made fast only when co-located, let go where a tug is lost, claims.
- **Transwarp:** the accepted conduit rules.
- **Tactical handoff:** the existing scene handoff.
- **Identity and salvage:** the persistent hull identity and the ordinary salvage rules.

It is not yard repair under another name:
- no yard, market, licence, production or refit;
- the hulk is never instantly operational;
- no new hull is ever made.

## 1. Core rule and recovery lifecycle (§1, §6, §14)

A hulk does not become operational on its own. It gets a persistent recovery objective (`db.borg.recoveries`), one per hulk, from the day it lies stranded:

```
disabled_assimilated → recovery_pending → recovery_en_route → under_tow → at_borg_node → regenerating → operational
```

| Phase | What holds | Leaves when |
|---|---|---|
| `disabled_assimilated` | an assimilated Borg hull, disabled, lying free, its drones aboard (`stranded.crew === 'assimilated'`) | the same day it opens: → `recovery_pending` |
| `recovery_pending` | waiting, with the reason on record: no vessel free, the vessel resting (§5), recoveries already under way, too far, cooldown after a failed attempt, no node in reach | a vessel is sent → `recovery_en_route`; it lies at a valid node → `at_borg_node` (taken in where it lies); budget spent → closed |
| `recovery_en_route` | the vessel's outbound journey (conduit or conventional, §4); the hulk has not moved | the vessel stands in the hulk's system and makes it fast → `under_tow` |
| `under_tow` | the tow journey home, conventional only, at the tow speed; the hulk still disabled | delivered at the node → `at_borg_node`; vessel lost → closed (the hulk where it was let go) |
| `at_borg_node` | the node has it (`h.borgRegen`); no yard, no repair queue | next day → `regenerating` |
| `regenerating` | +2% of max hull a day while it lies at a node that is still valid; status `repairing`, never `ready` | the operating threshold (60%) → `operational` |
| `operational` | the same hull back in service under the Collective, `status: ready`, `h.regenerated = { day, node, days, from }` | — (closed `hulk_operational`) |

**Ends** (`result.result`); every objective ends in exactly one:

| Result | When |
|---|---|
| `hulk_operational` | back in service at the threshold |
| `awaiting_drones` | regenerated, but its crew is under the operating minimum (§8) |
| `recovery_vessel_destroyed` | the vessel was lost; the hulk lies where it was let go |
| `hulk_destroyed` | the hulk was lost or is no longer the Collective's |
| `cancelled` | the doctrine was switched off (`releaseBorg`) |
| `no_reachable_node` | no node within the bounded tow for `searchDays`; the hulk is abandoned |
| `abandoned` | after `recoveryMaxAttempts` failed tows, or when the pending budget (`recoveryPendingDays`) runs out |

There is no immortal objective: every wait is bounded by the pending budget. Closed records are history (the newest 30 kept).

**Fields:** `id`, `hullId` and `vid` (the hulk), `tug` (vessel vid), `phase` and `history`, `route` (outbound and inbound), `at` (current system), `tow` (mission state, attachment), `node`, `regen` (state, progress, days), `movement`, `attempts`, `waitReason`, `result`.

The shared mission record carries the rest (journeys, ETA, losses), and the objective names it: `missionId` and `missions`.

## 2. The same hull (§2, §24)

The record that was assimilated is the record that is towed, regenerated and returned:
- same `identity.vid`, class (`shipId`), origin (`identity.origin.builder`) and `assimilated`;
- the identity history carries `assimilated` (and `abandoned` if the Collective lets it go);
- the hull it is delivered with is the hull it starts regenerating from.

Nothing is spawned or copied. In game the Inquiry `br-main:hull:40` is assimilated at 706/7069 hull. It is towed at 706 and leaves regeneration at 4382 (62%): the same vid, class 289, origin Terran, one record carrying that vid.

## 3. Capability in canonical data (§3)

A Borg recovery vessel is a class whose data says `borgRecovery: true`; capacity is the shared `recoveryCapacity` (≥ the hulk's mass).

| Class | Flag | Capacity | Recovers? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 346 Borg Assimilation Cube | `borgRecovery: true` | 10 (authored, `recoveryCapacityNote`) | yes |
| 261 Borg Tactical Cube | none | 6 | no (deferred) |
| any assimilated foreign hull | its class's own (none has the flag) | — | no, never automatically |

The manifest and runtime ship data carry the same entry: `check:ship-manifest` passes and `validate:ships` is 14/14. The Inquiry (289) has mass 7, within the Cube's 10.

## 4. Movement (§4, §10)

**Out to the casualty.** The vessel flies the shared journey system: legs, its class speed, crossings and blockades, save/load.

It may go by conduit only when:
- the vessel itself is transwarp-capable;
- the objective permits it (`recoveryTranswarp`);
- the accepted conduit rules allow it: off cooldown, condition, a worthwhile saving (`B.chooseMovement`).

Otherwise it flies conventionally. A recovery never waits for a conduit. The choice and its reason go on the mission (`borgMovement`) and the transwarp log.

**Home with the hulk.** Always conventional: the ordinary journey at the shared tow speed.

**Measured in game:**
- the Cube from Vex to the hulk at Harman: by conduit, 2 days against 19 conventional ("saves 17 days");
- the tow home Harman → Kardon → Vex: legs of 12 + 15 days at 0.92× (1.32 × 0.70);
- from Rator, the tow runs Rator → Virinat → Harman → Kardon → Vex: four legs, 56 days.

## 5. Towing (§5)

It is the shared tow mission (`requestHullRecovery` / `startOutbound` / `onRecoveryScene` / `startTow` / `deliverHulk` / `dropTowedHulk`), not a Borg engine:
- **Made fast** only when the vessel stands in the hulk's system: `onRecoveryScene`, the shared claim verdict.
- **The tow speed:** `SPD.towSpeed(groupSpeed, book.config.towPenalty)`, penalty 0.70.
- **The hulk stays disabled** throughout the tow.
- **Interception:** the tow runs blockades through the shared engine (`runJourneyBlockades`) with the hulk as a towed member, and escorts may fly with it. Where the captain is, the interception is his scene (§11).
- **If the vessel is lost,** the tow is let go there (`dropUntowed`): the hulk lies at the system it actually reached.

**What the provider adds** (`registerRecoveryNodes('borg', …)`):

| Hook | What it does |
|---|---|
| `nodeOk` | where a tow may deliver: a Borg node (§7), never a yard |
| `receive` | delivery: the node takes the hulk in (`borgRegen`) |
| `inNode` | the hulk is at a node: no other claim, no ordinary yard intake |
| `outbound` | the conduit choice out (§4) |
| `afterPhase` | the shared record's after-delivery phases, read off the Borg lifecycle |

A provider polity is never taken in by an ordinary yard. The generic AI tug planner leaves it alone.

**Bounds:**
- at most `recoveryMaxActive` (2) recoveries under way at once;
- a recovery longer than `recoveryMaxTowDays`, out and home, is not sent;
- after a failed tow, the next waits `recoveryCooldownDays`;
- a hulk is abandoned after `recoveryMaxAttempts` (2) failed tows.

**The duty bound.** A recovery vessel rests after each recovery for as long again. It spends at most `recoveryDutyShare` (50%) of its days on recoveries, and is free for assimilation the whole time. This is the answer to "the Cube spends all its time towing" (§17).

**Assimilation comes first.** The daily pass selects targets before it dispatches recoveries.

**The shared AI tow switch governs it.** The Collective's tows are AI tows on the shared mission, so campaign config `aiRecovery: false` (the switch the quiet scenarios of the towing, treaty and cleanup gates use) sends no Borg vessel either. The objective waits, saying why, and its budget runs. A hulk lying at a node is still taken in, because that is no tow.

## 6. Regeneration (§6, §15, §16)

At a node, from the day after delivery, a hulk gains `regenSharePerDay` (2%) of its class's max hull a day, while it lies at a node that is still valid. Leaving or losing the node holds it.

At `regenTargetShare` (60%, the doctrine's own ready threshold) it is back in service. From a disabled hull at 10%, that takes about 25 days: 26 in game, 25–27 across the long run. It is never `ready` before.

## 7. Node eligibility rules (§9)

`nodeEligibility({ controller, origin, power, assimilatedDay, day, blockaded })`:

| World | Node? | Why (as the debug shows it) |
|---|---|---|
| not held by the Collective | no | "not held by the Collective" |
| held, under an applicable blockade | no | "under blockade" |
| held, origin Borg (Vex) | yes, always | "a Collective world" (`origin`) |
| held, assimilated, before `assimilatedDay + nodeStabilizeDays` | no | "stabilizing until day N" |
| held, assimilated, after that | yes | "a stabilized assimilated world" (`assimilated`) |
| held without an assimilation record | no | "no Borg infrastructure" |

**Stabilization:** `nodeStabilizeDays` is 30, scaled by the chart. In game the scale is 2, so 60 days. `assimilatedDay` is the newest completion record for that world.

**A hulk already lying at a valid node** is taken in where it lies: no vessel, no tow. A hulk at a world that is not yet a node waits, or is towed to one.

**In game:**
- Tarellia, assimilated by the cube on day 48, is "stabilizing until day 108". An Inquiry lying there is not regenerated: its hull is unchanged and nothing takes it in.
- After day 108 a second Inquiry there is taken in on its first day, and regenerates 706 → 847 → 989 → 1130.

## 8. Transwarp / tow interaction matrix (§10, §19)

| Movement | Conduit? | How it is enforced |
|---|---|---|
| vessel out to the casualty, vessel transwarp-capable, conduit rules allow | **yes** | provider `outbound` → `startJourney({ mode: 'transwarp' })` |
| vessel out, vessel not capable (e.g. a recovery-capable sphere) | no: conventional, leg by leg | `transwarpCapable` false → "no transwarp capability in the force" |
| vessel out, conduit on cooldown / vessel damaged / saving too small | no: conventional (a recovery never waits) | `transwarpRefusal` / `chooseMovement` |
| objective forbids it (`recoveryTranswarp: false`) | no | "the objective does not permit a conduit" |
| the tow home (vessel + hulk) | **never** | `startTow` asks for an ordinary journey; `startTranswarpJourney` refuses any spec with a tow: "a tow cannot open a conduit" |
| a hulk carried through a conduit by a capable vessel | **never** | the same refusal; carrier/conduit access is carried forward |
| the regenerated hull afterwards | only if its own class can (an Inquiry cannot) | the class data; regeneration gives nothing |

## 9. No market or yard semantics (§7)

Through the whole loop:
- no yard repairs anything: no repair day on any book, no `recovering` / `in_yard` intake for the Collective;
- no market, sale, production right, licence or refit;
- no world changes hands, and no capture or occupation is recorded.

## 10. Crew and drones (§8): the choice

There is no drone economy. The one bounded rule:
- **Recovered:** only a hulk whose drones are aboard (`stranded.crew === 'assimilated'`).
- **Crewless hulk:** left to the ordinary stranded rules, so it is written off as a derelict, as on e949936.
- **Crew below the operating minimum at the threshold** (`minOperatingCrew`, 0.5): it stays non-operational, `awaiting_drones`, and the objective closes. No drones are made this round.

## 11. Tactical handoff (§11, §23)

Where the captain stands at a crossing the tow makes, the shared handoff holds the interception for his scene:
- **One encounter.** The vessel spawns as the runner (its own vid). The hulk is a towed participant (`towed: true`), never spawned: no tow-line physics.
- **While it is held,** nothing strategic touches either hull.
- **The scene's result** is written back to the same records.

In game:
- With the cube destroyed in the scene, the encounter resolved once (no fallback, no second encounter).
- The cube is lost on its own record.
- The hulk lies at Virinat, where the tow was let go, still disabled and the Collective's. The objective is closed `recovery_vessel_destroyed`.

The gate deals the cube's last damage through the scene engine's damage path (`damageCombatTarget`); see §16.

## 12. Salvage and abandonment (§12)

- **While a Borg recovery is active,** an ordinary salvage claim is refused: "already claimed". A hulk crewed by its drones is no free salvage either, as before.
- **When the Collective abandons a hulk** (attempts, budget, no node in reach), it is written off where it lies, as any owner writes one off:
  - `status: lost`, `fate: written_off`, `h.derelict = { abandonedBy: 'borg', reason, … }`;
  - identity history `assimilated` → `abandoned`.
  
  Then the ordinary salvage rules apply: a Terran claim on it is allowed, kind `salvage`.

## 13. Hirogen (§13)

The Hirogen ignore an assimilated disabled wreck (accepted), and now also one regenerating at a node. Once it is operational again it is seen like any vessel: in the pure check, the hunter records it on the day it returns to service.

## 14. Debug (§15)

The Borg debug section has an "Assimilated hulk recovery" block (`[data-hulk-recovery-block]`).

**Header:** the rules, then the counts — recoveries, completed, abandoned, vessel losses, returned to service.

**One row per hulk:**
- vid and class;
- hull % and where (or "→ next system" under tow);
- recovery status and why it waits;
- recovery vessel;
- movement (mode, conventional / transwarp days, why);
- tow (state, tow speed, route);
- node;
- regeneration (state, progress, days);
- ETA;
- result.

**Borg nodes table:** world, node yes/no and kind, why.

## 18. Findings to note (not changed)

- **No power can blockade a Borg node.** Nobody is formally at war with the Collective, so a blockade of Vex is refused. A node is lost only if its world changes hands, and nothing retakes an assimilated world yet (carried forward). Unreachability is therefore distance: a node whose tow alone is longer than `recoveryMaxTowDays` (pure 21).
- **Interdiction needs a blockade on a held world.** Near Vex, most tows cross unaffiliated systems (Kardon, Harman) or pirate space, and neither can be blockaded. The gate uses Rator → Virinat (Romulan).
- **A blockade far from its power withdraws in 10 days unsupplied**, so the gate holds it on station for the scenario.
- **The Collective's tows appear in `book.hullRecoveries`** beside everyone else's, as they should on the shared mission. The first full-suite pass on this branch showed what that means for the accepted towing-audit gate: its fixture reads "the newest mission" (`hullRecoveries.slice(-1)`) and quiets AI tows with `aiRecovery: false`, which the Borg tows did not yet honour, so a Borg tow became "the newest" mid-scenario (`RECOVERY-FLOW`: `Cannot read properties of null (reading 'eta')`). The Borg tows now honour the switch (above). The accepted gate is unchanged and passes 6/6.
- **Interdicted scenes resolve by the scene's own rules.** The gate forces the cube's loss, because the cube (30 084 hull) is rarely lost to an interception on its own: in the pure fixture it took six heavy blockaders.
- **After assimilating a world, the cube withdraws home conventionally** (Tarellia → Vex took it out of reach for about 90 days). A hulk at the new world waits for it, or for the world to stabilize.
